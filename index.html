Who is Wayne Shen

Real recipes. Surreal delivery.

foodiechina888 is Wayne Shen — a content creator who turned a cooking channel into something far stranger and more beloved: a deadpan, satirical universe where grandma's cleaver meets water from Mississippi River, the meat always gets a Thai massage, and every dish ends the same way.
